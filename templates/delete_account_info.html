❌ How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account from within WhatsApp. Deleting your account is irreversible. We can't restore your account if deleted accidentally.

Note: You must be logged in on your primary device to delete your account. This cannot be done from a linked device.

📱 Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open WhatsApp.
  2. Tap More Options > Settings > Account > Delete my account.
  3. Enter your phone number in full international format.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Select a reason for deletion from the dropdown.
  6. Tap Delete account again to confirm.

🧾 What Happens When You Delete

  • Deletes your account and removes it from all devices.
  • Erases your entire message history.
  • Removes you from all WhatsApp groups.
  • Deletes any Google Account backup linked to WhatsApp.
  • Removes you as a channel admin or follower (but not your past activity).
  • Deletes any channel you created, if there are no other admins.

⏳ After Deletion

  • You cannot recover your account.
  • It may take up to 90 days to fully erase your data from backups.
  • Your information may still exist in logs (disconnected from your identity).
  • Legal, security, or terms violation data may be retained.
  • Your shared info with other Meta companies will also be deleted.

📢 Channels

  • If you're a channel owner, delete your channel first before deleting your account.
  • If no other admins exist, your channel will be permanently deleted.
  • Channel updates remain visible to followers even after deletion.
  • If there are other admins, ownership transfers to the longest-following admin.

📌 Final Notes

  • Ask your contacts to restart WhatsApp if they still see you as a user.
  • This won’t delete their existing chat with you, only removes your profile from their contacts.
